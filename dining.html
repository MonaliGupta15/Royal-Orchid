Tiger Trail — Authentic Frontier & Mughlai Delicacies

Inspired by the ancient culinary routes of the North-West Frontier Province, Tiger Trail is celebrated for its slow-cooked delicacies, fragrant biryanis, and tender kebabs prepared in traditional live charcoal tandoors.

Signature Kakori & Galouti Kebabs: Melt-in-mouth textures infused with 24 proprietary royal spices.
Romantic Starlit Gazebos: Private candlelit dining surrounded by lush lawns and gentle water bodies.
Sikandari Raan & Dal Tiger Trail: Slow-simmered for 18 hours for deep, velvety richness.
